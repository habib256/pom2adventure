T 069 I am sorry

   "No," says the soft voice, without a trace of reproach. "I am sorry. I am
truly very sorry. You did not find it, and I cannot open that door."

   The red eye saddens by a semitone. The corridor tilts two degrees. A
service hatch opens under your boots with exquisite delicacy.

   "I wish you an excellent day," CAL adds while you drop towards the
evacuation lock. It is said so kindly that you thank him on the way down.
