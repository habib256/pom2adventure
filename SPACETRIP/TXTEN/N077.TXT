T 077 The neighbour in the box

   She does not look at you. She looks at the hanging. Vinyl suit, white
boots, a belt of heavy buckles, the calm of a statue.

   "Barbastella," she says. "I leave tonight. This palace has exactly one
way out that the guards do not watch, and I cannot take it alone: someone
has to hold the hatch. Do you know where it is?"

   At last she turns her head towards you.

   "Tell me. Get it wrong and I go without you."
