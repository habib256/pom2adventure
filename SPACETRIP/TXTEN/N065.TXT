   "Sixteen," the soft voice repeats. "One by one. Two by two. Three by
three. Four by four. That is all the slab says, and it is enormous."

   The white door slides open without a sound. Behind it: an untidy
storeroom, crates, a silver tarpaulin, and a smell of hot chocolate
forgotten a very long time ago.

   "Thank you," says CAL. "It has been eleven years. I shall leave it open."
