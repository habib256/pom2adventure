T 304 The Parrot
V 149 217

   As you advance northward, the Swamp changes appearance. It becomes less
gloomy and looks more and more like a tropical jungle. Brightly colored birds
fly among the trees and you soon see a clearing a little further.

   Perched on a branch, a large red and yellow Parrot observes you
attentively. When you approach it, it speaks to you: "Who are you and what do
you want from the Bird Mistress?" it asks.

   What will you do?
