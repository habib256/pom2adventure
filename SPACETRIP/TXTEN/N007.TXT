T 007 The Replay

   The autopilot of the Convertible has exactly one virtue: it never
leaves you behind. It scoops you up, flies you home, drops you in the seat
and turns the music off.

   You are not dead. You are worse: you are on replay. The live feed
caught everything, the Holophone did not shake once, and the part where
you scream while spinning on your own axis is already cut into a loop.

   Your followers loved it. There are thirteen of them now.

   You still have all your gear. Nothing is ever lost in this job, except
face.
