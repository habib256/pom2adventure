T 022 The Corridor Called Grey

   -- Move along, says the trouper without looking up.

   You move along. The armour is two sizes too big; you walk like a broken
elevator. The corridor is grey, the carpet is grey, the air is grey. On one
bulkhead, a service notice nobody has read in ten years:

   WASTE CHUTES. HATCH ONE: plasma furnace. HATCH TWO: ventilation,
   cold draught. HATCH THREE: secondary grinder.

   A trouper walks past shivering. "Number two is blowing again," he
grumbles. "We have been freezing at that post since Monday."

   Boots. A patrol is coming. A great many boots.
