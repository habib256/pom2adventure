T 128 The Price of Defeat

   You are skillful, but the Brigand Chief is a formidable swordsman. Soon,
his blade grazes your ribs: you have lost.

   He congratulates you however for your bravery and spreads his cloak so you
can sit down.

   "Let's get to our bargain," he says. "What have I won?"

   You must give him a Magic Stone, a jewel or any other object.

   If you have nothing to hand over except, perhaps, your Copper Ring:

CB 0 180 Admit you cannot pay
CB 1 407 Hand over a possession from your bag
