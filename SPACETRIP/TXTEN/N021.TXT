T 021 The Service Airlock

   You wave. You smile. You explain that you are with the interstellar press
and that this piece is going to change their careers. The two troupers confer
helmet to helmet, at length.

   -- That name is not on the list.
   -- The list is upside down.
   -- That name is not on the upside-down list either.

   They carry you, very politely, to a service airlock. They give you a
little wave. The vacuum takes you out by the boots.

   You keep filming anyway: the station, the stars, the soles of your own
boots turning through frame. Three hundred thousand views. One comment, from
your mother, who thinks you have lost weight.
