T 028 The Sword Trees

   Several SWORD TREES attack you as if they formed a single adversary. They
cannot see you and only locate themselves by the sounds you make. Each of them
bears several branches armed with sharp blades, and they converge in silence.
You have an advantage: they only detect sound. Show caution and precision in
your attack.

   Face them by applying the combat rules from the book. If you defeat them,
continue reading at the indicated number. Otherwise, your corpse will serve as
fertilizer for the swamp.
