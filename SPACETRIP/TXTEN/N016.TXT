T 016 What happens next

   The glass was not yours. It knows before you do: it goes very cold, or
very hot, or very silent, and the whole room turns its head as one.

   A Klinger stands up. He never needed a reason, only a beginning. The
second one stands up because the first one did. The band changes tune,
which proves it knew a second one all along.

   You go over backwards across a table with the Holophone held out at
arm's length, still filming, because that is the job. The shot is
magnificent. The ceiling of the Cantina is corrugated iron.
