T 050 Arrakoss, under two suns

   The Convertible lands in a fan of sand. You fold the roof back -- that is
the whole point of the model -- and a lungful of air at sixty degrees slaps
you like a hot towel.

   Two suns. Two shadows. No clouds. Sand to the horizon, and the horizon is
made of sand as well.

   A caped shape peels away from a rock and walks over without hurrying. An
old nomad, eyes blue right through, a tortoise sort of smile.

   "You," he says. "You want to cross. Everybody wants to cross."

C 057 Hear the old nomad out
C 051 Set straight off across the sea of sand
C 003 Close the roof and leave
