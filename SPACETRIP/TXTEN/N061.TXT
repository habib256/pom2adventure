T 061 Mopey blows his nose

   You unfold the bath towel and lay it across his knees. The robot looks at
it for six full seconds -- an eternity, for him.

   "A towel. Nobody has ever given me a towel." He blows his nose in it,
loudly, which raises several questions of anatomy, then folds it into eight
and keeps it.

   "I remain profoundly miserable," he points out, shifting against the
wall. "But do go through. Really. Go through."
