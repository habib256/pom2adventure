T 180 The Brigand's Honor

   You show your empty bag to the Brigand Chief. You have no possession to
hand over. If you still wear the Copper Ring, you reluctantly offer it to him,
but he refuses to take it.

   "Keep what you have left," he says. "You have shown honesty and courage. I
release you from your payment."

   He gives you a friendly pat on the shoulder, and you feel mutual respect
being born.
