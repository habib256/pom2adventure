T 038 You Already Have It

   You raise the Holophone out of reflex, then stop.

   So does the creature. It freezes in the light, turns its head a little,
opens the double jaw three-quarters on -- its good side -- and waits.

   You show it the screen: the shot is already there, already posted, already
commented on four thousand times. It looks for a long moment. It closes the
jaw. That is very nearly disappointment.

   Then it remembers what it is, hisses, and drops down an air vent, taking a
fire extinguisher with it.

   -- Move, says Ripleigh. It is sulking.
