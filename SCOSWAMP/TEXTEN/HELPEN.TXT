THE KEYS
  [A-E] Scene choice (never a command)
  [SPACE/RETURN/ESC] Text, HGR picture, then picture and text
  [I] Bag: items, Magic Stones and amulets; [M] Map of the Swamp
  [S]/[L] Saved games (write / resume); [H] help; [Q] quit

MAP
  One cell per clearing, empty until you have set foot in it
  (nn) seen; <nn> you are here; -- path walked; -? path known, not yet taken
  [M]/[ESC] closes it; without the Copper Ring, compasses lose their north

SAVED GAMES
  [0-9] Slot; each game carries the title of the page it stands on
  Saved state: scene, hero, items, amulets, visits, monsters, clearing
  A missing, incomplete or corrupt file is rejected
AMULETS: Wolf, Flower, Bird, Spider, Frog; the fake Bird stays a thing apart
BAG: [A-L] picks a Stone; [I] or [ESC] closes; commands are suspended
FIGHT: [SPACE] attack; [C] Luck; [F] Flee; [I] bag; [M] map
