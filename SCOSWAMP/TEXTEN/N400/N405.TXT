T 405 The Fall into the Mud

   Your foot sinks into the mud, you fall heavily and twist your arm. This bad
fall costs you 1 SKILL point.

   Gathering your wits, you rise with difficulty, mud clinging to your
clothes, and wade your way to the other side.

   Three directions then open before you. To the west, a dark path disappears
behind damp trunks. To the east, a lighter trail winds through tall grass. To
the south, the passage you arrived by remains usable.
