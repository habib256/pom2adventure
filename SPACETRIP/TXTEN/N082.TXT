T 082 The sorting of Trolley

   The little robot lifts its binoculars towards you, swivels them, and
emits a beep that sounds a great deal like joy. It taps three bins:
METAL. ORGANIC. PRECIOUS.

   Then it sets three things in front of you. A rusted spring. A
fossilised sandwich, hard as a roof tile. A Martian fruit core, its pips
still damp.

   It beeps out a rule, slowly, so that you understand: here, PRECIOUS
does not mean expensive. Precious means what is still alive. In a dump
everything else can be replaced.

   It waits. It has all eternity and you have only the live feed.
