T 094 Fourth Legend: The Glowing Finger

   The photo is blurred, badly framed, taken at floor level: a very small
grey alien, one finger lit like a night light, an ancient telephone against
its ear, and an expression nobody has ever managed to name.

   -- Did he get through? asks the Sponsor, and his voice changes a little.
Good. Good. Going home. It is nearly always that, in the end.

   A pause that lasts much too long.

   -- Four of five. One more, and I show you who I am. You will be
disappointed. Then you will be very, very surprised.
