T 017 The glass that sings

   The glass sings one more note, then stops. The barman shrugs all four
shoulders. "Drink. It is green, it is warm, it is legal here."

   It is vile. You film your own face doing it: the finest shot of your
career.

   "You are looking for something," says the barman, polishing a glass
that did not exist a moment ago. "So keep listening. The smuggler sells
anything to anyone. And his copilot has been limping since this morning.
He will not say so. Those two never say anything. Look at his paw."

   He pours you a second glass. This one does not sing. It hisses.
