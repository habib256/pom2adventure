T 034 The Hold

   The airlock opens onto an enormous room, hot and wet, where long black
things hang from the ceiling in arches. The deck is covered with eggs the
size of armchairs.

   This is not the shuttle.

   It comes down a wall without a sound, twice your height, black, smooth,
eyeless, with a second jaw inside the first. It pins you gently to a
bulkhead. Then it looks at the eggs, then at you, then at the eggs.

   It gives you the tour. Every egg. One at a time. Like family photographs.
Ripleigh collects you an hour later and asks no questions about the drool.

C 007 Accept the escort back to the airlock
