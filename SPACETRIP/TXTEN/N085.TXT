T 085 The present of Trolley
V 089

   Trolley files each thing in its bin. It does this with a care that
makes no sense here and that, for that exact reason, makes a great deal.

   Then it rummages in its own belly, at length, with the sound of a
kitchen drawer, and brings out a flat battery still in its wrapper. It
holds it out with both hands. Its binoculars turn a full circle.

   You know the shape: it is a Holophone battery. You have never seen a
new one.

   It beeps once more and goes back to sorting. Somewhere in this dump
there is a pip that will grow, which is already more than most channels in
this galaxy will ever manage.
