T 087 The skip of dead channels

   A whole skip, just for this: ring lights gone dark, bent selfie sticks,
lapel microphones tangled up like seaweed.

   On a cracked screen one last video is looping. A man is smiling too
wide. "Hey everyone, big format change today!" The video is fourteen years
old. It has eleven views and two of them are his.

   You stay longer than you need to.

   Then you raise the Holophone, you film the skip, and you tell your
twelve followers that it is all right. They do not answer. They are
asleep. It is three in the morning on the only planet that watches you.
