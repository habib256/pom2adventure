T 081 The Orbital Dump

   A ring of rubbish as wide as a moon, in slow orbit around nothing. The
whole universe comes here to throw away what it regrets: ships, furniture,
fashions, dead channels.

   You land on a sheet of metal that may once have been a wing. Gravity is
soft. You bounce a little with every step, which makes everything more
solemn and a great deal more ridiculous.

   Three things stick out of the rubbish. A small compactor robot with
binocular eyes, sorting with the patience of a saint. A wellness pod
crushed like a can. And a white armour shell, sitting against a dead
engine in the posture of somebody who was waiting.
