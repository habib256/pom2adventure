T 084 The white armour
V 089

   The armour sits against a dead engine, helmet down, as though it had
been waiting eleven years for a relief shift. It is empty. That armour
always was a little empty.

   You take it apart piece by piece. The chest plate is scratched by
something that missed from a very long way off. The helmet gives a field
of view of twelve degrees, which explains an enormous amount.

   You put it on. The boots are too big, the gloves are too big,
everything is too big, and yet in a badly lit corridor nobody will see the
difference. Nobody ever looks at those people.

   You salute the shell. It does not answer.
