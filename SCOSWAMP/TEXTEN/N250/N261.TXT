T 261 Curse and Spider

   You throw your Curse against the Spider Master. His cry turns into
convulsions; he collapses, writhes and then rises in an abominable form: an
immense gray spider with giant body and man's head, eyes full of hatred.

   A cold runs through you - the Curse also affects you. Roll a die; you lose
as many STAMINA points as the number obtained. If you survive, the Giant
Spider attacks you. You cannot flee; you must fight to the death.
