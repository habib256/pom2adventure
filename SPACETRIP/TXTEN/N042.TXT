T 042 Teleported, more or less

   The world glitters, a soft hiss climbs, and there you are on a bright
disc in a white room. A very calm officer studies you. Pointed ears, one
eyebrow raised by a millimetre, the look of someone who finished the
arithmetic before you arrived.

   "Welcome aboard. Transfer successful at ninety-two per cent."

   You look down. Your left boot is still on the planet below. The foot is
here. The eyebrow climbs a second millimetre.

   "Fascinating," he says.

C 043 Limp after him with as much dignity as you can
C 047 Demand your boot back from the transporter first
