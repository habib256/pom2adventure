T 043 Mr. Spork's riddle

   Two identical androids guard two doors. The officer explains, hands
behind his back: "One of them always tells the truth. The other always lies.
Only one door leads to the bridge. You are allowed ONE question, put to ONE
of them. Then you pick a door."

   The two androids smile in exactly the same way. It is unpleasant.

   "Take your time," the officer adds. "A reasonable amount of it."
