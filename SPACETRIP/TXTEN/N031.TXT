T 031 Here, Puss, Puss

   The cat looks at you. You crouch. You make the noise with your mouth, the
one that always works and never works. The cat blinks, turns on its heel and
vanishes up the duct at a scandalous speed.

   You run after it shouting "puss" through an abandoned freighter. It is an
excellent way to get noticed.

   The tubes go out. They do not come back on. Something very large and very
smooth straightens up right behind you, with a sound of slow dripping, and
you do not even have time to frame the shot.

   The Holophone falls and films the ceiling for forty minutes. It is your
best-rated video to date.
