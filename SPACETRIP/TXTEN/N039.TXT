T 039 The Escape Shuttle

   Ripleigh slams the hatch, hits the control three times, insults the
manufacturer, and the shuttle pops off the Nostalgo with the sound of a jar
being opened.

   Through the port, the old freighter falls away: a dead city, long as a
boulevard, with one single light still flickering, once every three seconds.

   Joanesy settles on the console, precisely on the controls, exactly as the
law requires.

   -- Do you film everything? asks Ripleigh.
   -- Otherwise it does not count, you say.
   -- On that ship, she says, nothing ever counted.
