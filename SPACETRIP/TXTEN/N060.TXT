T 060 Station CAL 9000

   The airlock shuts behind you on a white curved corridor, frighteningly
clean. Far down at the end, a red eye set into a panel watches you without
blinking.

   Much nearer, a large robot sits across the passage with his head in his
hands. He fills the whole corridor.

   "Don't mind me," he says in a voice like the end of the world. "I have a
brain the size of a planet and they ask me to hold a door. Would you like me
to tell you about it? It takes three hours. Nobody has ever lasted."
