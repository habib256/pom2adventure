T 001 SPACE TRIP -- Part One

   Twelve followers. Your mother, an advertising bot, and ten souls whose
loneliness has made them generous. That is @CosmoSelfie, your channel, the
faintest glow in the galaxy.

   This morning the Holophone rang: the Great Galactic Selfie Challenge is
open. The first creator to bring back a selfie with FIVE legends of the
universe wins a million followers and the contract of the mysterious
Vortex Sponsor.

   Outside on the launch deck the Convertible is waiting: a chrome rocket
with fins, and a roof that opens. In vacuum. It is a stupid idea. It is
your ship.

   Three berths along, Bizz Lightspan waves at his forty million
followers. He has not seen you. Nobody has ever seen you.

   It starts now.
