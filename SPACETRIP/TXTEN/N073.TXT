T 073 The sand of the arena

   Two guards in finned helmets take you by an elbow each, lift you over a
balustrade without effort, and let go.

   The sand is hot. The stands are full. Opposite, a gate rises on a
three-horned beast which clearly has an opinion about you.

   You raise the Holophone. The live feed explodes: forty thousand viewers
and, for the first time in your career, genuine suspense.

   What follows is confused, very fast, and ends in a net. They drop you off
outside your ship with a cloakroom ticket.
