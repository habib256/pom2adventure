T 027 One Flash Too Many

   You raise the Holophone and the ring light detonates in white.

   Lord Vapour does not move. He simply lifts two fingers. Your collar closes
by itself. Your feet leave the deck. You rise, slowly, turning, like a party
balloon at the end of a very long evening.

   -- I did warn you. Kuh-hooo. I thought I was clear.

   He holds you up there while four troupers come in, film the whole thing on
their own devices and leave laughing. Then he drops you, and two guards carry
you to the airlock.

   Your live hits its record. The title: "Somebody Used The Flash".
