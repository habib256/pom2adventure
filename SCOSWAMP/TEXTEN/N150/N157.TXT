T 157 The Sword Trees Clearing
V 279 022

   The path narrows worryingly and you fear reaching a dead end. A few meters
further, it widens and you enter a very small clearing. The place is
surrounded by strangely shaped trees: small, dark green, with branches
resembling arms.

   Soon, you notice that each arm holds a sword at its end. These trees move
and converge toward you. You are attacked by SWORD TREES! They encircle you
and prepare their assault.
