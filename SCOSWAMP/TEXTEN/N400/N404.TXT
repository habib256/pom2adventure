T 404 On the Other Side

   You reach the other side safe and sound. Insects swirl around you, but the
mud did not claim you.

   Three directions then open before you, each leading into the unknown. To
the west, a dark path disappears behind damp trunks. To the east, a lighter
trail seems to wind through tall grass. To the south, the passage you arrived
by remains usable.
