T 355 Return to Courbensaule

   The path leading to Courbensaule is now familiar to you, but when you enter
the town again, something seems to have changed. You indeed feel people
looking at you and murmurs rising as you pass. You then understand the rumor
of your exploits has spread around and they're now publicly known.

   You feel a certain unease being thus observed and, as you engage in a side
street, you have the impression of being followed. You immediately turn around
to find yourself face to face with two ragged CUTPURSES. They attack you
immediately, convinced you must have brought back a fabulous treasure from
your wanderings in the Swamp.

   You can't flee and you don't have enough time to resort to magic. All
that's left is to fight them...
