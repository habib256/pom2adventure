T 373 Surprise Attack

   Test your Luck. If you're Lucky, your attack is so lightning-fast you
manage to wound the sorcerer; you will then subtract 2 points from the total
STAMINA of your opponent before combat engages.

   If you're Unlucky, you don't manage to hit him; in that case, you'll also
go to 225 to fight him, but without changing this time the total STAMINA he
has.
