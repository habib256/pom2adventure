T 055 Riding down

   Getting off a worm as long as a train means three hundred metres of slide
on warm scales. You scream. The camera shakes. It is the finest shot you
have ever taken.

   The Worm sets you down near the Convertible, breathes out a cloud of
cinnamon and dives again without a word, having none.

   The old nomad watches from a distance. He says nothing. He nods once,
which for him is presumably a standing ovation.
