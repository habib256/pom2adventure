T 051 Three ways to walk

   The sea of sand starts right there, warm and soft, and the first step
sinks to the ankle. Three kilometres of dune lie between you and the black
rock sticking up at the far end.

   You hear nothing at all. That is exactly what is worrying: something
underneath is listening.

   You must pick a way of setting down your feet, and stick to it.
