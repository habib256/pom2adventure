T 072 A present for the Emperor

   You set the vial of amber spice on the cushion a servant slips under your
hands. Mingus lifts it against the light, turns it, breathes in a trace of
it and closes his eyes.

   "Spice. Real Spice." He snaps his fingers. "This stranger is my guest!
Box of honour! Cushions! Small fruit!"

   Twelve courtiers applaud with exactly the same smile. You film the throne,
the beard, the fan collar. Three followers come back.

   "We are going to watch some fighting," the Emperor adds. "I adore
fighting."
