T 099 Nobody Around To See It

   You glance back over your shoulder out of reflex, the way you look for a
witness.

   The hangar is empty. No white and red rocket, no jutting chin, no twelve
cameramen. Nowhere in the universe is Bizz Lightspan watching this happen to
you, and that leaves a small ridiculous pang you will admit to nobody.

   You raise the Holophone towards the blue cabin and press.

   Twelve followers. Twelve people in the whole universe get to see this
first. Which is, all things considered, quite a decent statistic.
