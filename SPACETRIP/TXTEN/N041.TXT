T 041 Three days of drifting

   The Surprise stops answering. It does not answer the second hail either,
nor the seventeenth. It banks slowly, lights its engines and leaves for
somewhere you are not.

   You drift three days in its wake, narrating the void. It is the longest
and flattest live show in galactic history. One follower unsubscribes. It was
the bot.

   In the end the Convertible's autopilot takes pity on you.
