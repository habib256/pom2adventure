T 419 Who You Are

   You are a fearless adventurer whom no peril has ever turned back, and wise
enough never to have ventured into the Scorpion Swamp. No one has ever managed
to map it: its paths mislead, the fog hides the sky, and compasses lose north
there.

   Then fate stepped in. On the King's Road you helped up an old woman, you
carried her into the shade, and she gave you a circle of copper with no
engraving. An hour later the ring had fitted itself to your finger.

   As long as you wear it you know where north lies and you never lose your
way. And it grows warm whenever you stand before an evil being, even one who
makes a great show of friendship. Warm before Evil, cold before Good.

   That is enough to walk into the Swamp. What remains is why: at Marshville,
the last township before the low lands, three men are looking for an
adventurer bold enough to risk it.
