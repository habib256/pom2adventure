T 008 The rules of the Great Challenge

   Rule one: a selfie counts if it shows your face AND the legend. A photo
of the back of your head does not count. A photo of a closed door does not
count. A drawing does not count; somebody tried.

   Rule two: each legend counts once. The jury has seen fourteen selfies
of the same sand worm from fourteen angles, and the jury is tired.

   Rule three: gear picked up along the way scores no points, but without
it you will not get through a single door. One towel, one battery, one
sack of cat kibble and one oversized suit of armour have already done more
for this galaxy than every space ranger put together.

   Rule four: the Vortex Sponsor only answers a complete channel.
