T 052 The sand opens

   The ground starts to beat. Not your heart: the ground. A ridge as long as
a freight train drives straight at you and the sand funnels away under your
boots.

   You have time to film a round jaw lined with hooks, wide as a hangar. The
framing is excellent. The light is superb.

   Then it is very dark, it smells of cinnamon, and it is extremely slippery.

   The Worm spits you out three dunes further on, covered in drool and
disgrace. The Convertible's autopilot finds you by smell.
