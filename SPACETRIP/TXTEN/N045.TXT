T 045 Illogical, but fascinating
V 046

   The right door opens onto the bridge: swivel chairs, a giant screen,
lights blinking in an order nobody has ever bothered to explain.

   "Your reasoning was correct," the officer says. You produce the
Holophone. He studies its ring light the way one studies a rare insect.

   "A portrait of oneself, taken by oneself, intended for people one has
never met." The eyebrow rises. "Illogical. But fascinating."

   He does not smile. He never smiles. The shot is perfect.
