T 015 The paw of Chewpaca
V 019

   You hold out your hand. Hans Lonely says "Bad idea" with the calm of a
man who has watched this bad idea before.

   Chewpaca looks at you for a long time. Then he lays his paw in your
hand, and it is huge, and it is warm, and it is astonishingly light. A
sliver of polished chrome is wedged between two pads: a piece of a camera
drone, dropped by a crew running in slow motion.

   You pull. The howl brings down two bottles and one Klinger.

   Then the copilot digs into his harness and presses a laminated card
into your hand: a press pass, still warm, with a chrome chin on the
photograph. He "found" it. Hans Lonely says nothing at all, which from him
is a compliment.
