T 066 The Suspicious Picnic
V 192 179

   You enter a pleasant clearing surrounded by gnarled oaks.

   A small man with a cheerful air is sitting on the ground, back leaning
against the trunk of a tree. He's eating cheese, a picnic basket open beside
him. He doesn't seem armed, except for a knife he uses to cut his portion.

   He notices you and greets you: "Hello, adventurer, would you like to share
my meal?" The Copper Ring diffuses a warmth around your finger that warns you:
don't trust him. Soon, you understand that he's a THIEF.

   What do you do?
