T 000 THE SCORPION SWAMP

   You are about to become an adventurer seeking glory.

   The Scorpion Swamp is a cursed place that no one dares explore. Monsters,
traps and sorcerers await you. Yet a fabulous treasure is said to lie in its
depths. Your decisions will make you a hero... or another victim.

   Before taking the first step, create your character. Dice will determine
SKILL, STAMINA and LUCK. Your Adventure Sheet will then remain at the top of
the screen. You will start with a sword, armour, a bag and a few Gold Pieces.

   This game is a homage to the gamebook by Steve Jackson, illustrated by
Duncan Smith (1984), French edition by Gallimard (1985).

   Commands:
 [A-E] - Choose   [L] - Load a game   [H] - Help   [Q] - Quit
