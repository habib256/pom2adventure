T 054 The Dune Worm wants a tag
V 056

   Tock. Tock. Tock. Three minutes later the dune heaves and the Worm comes
out of the sand thirty metres high, in a golden rain, jaw wide open.

   It stops. It listens to you filming. A worm with no eyes knows perfectly
well where a camera is.

   You jump onto its back, grab a scale the size of a door and raise the
Holophone. It poses. It really poses.

   Then it coughs, and a vial of amber spice rolls to your feet. A gift, or a
tip. With a worm you never can tell.
