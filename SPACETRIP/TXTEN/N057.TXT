T 057 What the old nomad says

   "Under the sand there is the Worm. It is as long as a train. It has no
eyes and it needs none: it listens."

   The nomad stamps his heel. Tock. Tock. Tock. "That is a beat. A beat
travels a long way through sand, and the Worm comes. Always."

   "So you walk WITHOUT a beat. One step, three steps, a halt, two steps, a
slide. Nothing that repeats. Nothing that can be counted. The Worm gets
bored and goes away."

   He pulls up his hood. "And the day you want to call it, you will know."
