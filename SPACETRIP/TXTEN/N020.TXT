T 020 The Gloom Star Hangar

   The Convertible sets down in a hangar the size of a cathedral, under a
thousand dead-eyed floodlights. Everywhere, soldiers in white armour march in
step and miss the turn. This is the Gloom Star, the worst-lit battle station
in the galaxy: no window, no reflection, not one decent key light. A
nightmare for a content creator.

seriousness. One of them is holding the list upside down.
