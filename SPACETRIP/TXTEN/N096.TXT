T 096 Bigger On The Inside

   You step in. You step out. You walk right round it from outside. You step
in again. It is bigger inside. Much bigger: a whole hall, a column in the
middle, and a handrail whose purpose you will understand later.

   The door shuts. Nobody touched it. The column starts rising and falling.
Doctor Which looks at the controls, frowns, and says something extremely
unpleasant:

   -- I am not the one flying.

   A date comes up. It is the wrong date. Under it, a universe number. That
one is wrong too. You raise the Holophone: twelve followers, perfect signal.

   TO BE CONTINUED: Part 2, the Universe Inside Out.
