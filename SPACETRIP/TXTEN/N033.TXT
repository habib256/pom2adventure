T 033 The Three Airlocks

   The passage ends at three round airlocks. Ripleigh sets the tracker on the
deck between them and takes a step back.

   -- Behind one of them is the escape shuttle. Behind the others is the
hold. Choose, and choose right: I open one door a night.

   PORT: beep... beep... beep... beep... Slow. Always on the beat.

   STARBOARD: beep. beep. beep-beep-beep-beepbeepbeep. Speeding up. Closing.

   CENTRE: beep....... beep... beep......... beep. Slow, but dragging,
stalling, starting again. Like something sleeping badly.

   The cat, in Ripleigh's arms, stares at the centre. Cats are always wrong.
