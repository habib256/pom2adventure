T 012 Hans Lonely and Chewpaca

   The smuggler in the waistcoat does not look up from his banknotes. "If
it is an autograph, that is fifty. If it is a passage, that is two
thousand and I do not ask what is in the crate."

   Beside him the hairy copilot occupies two chairs and part of a third.
He growls a sentence of three syllables. Hans Lonely translates without
turning round: "He says hello." The copilot growls fourteen more
syllables. "He says hello."

   Chewpaca is holding his left paw tight against his chest, very quietly,
the way you hold a thing that hurts when nobody is watching.

   Under the table sits a sack of cat kibble, enormous, absurd.
