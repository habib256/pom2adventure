T 146 Frog Combat

   Two enormous frogs attack you at the same time. To your great surprise,
their jaws are bristling with teeth...

   Each frog will fight you in turn at each assault. Before each assault, you
will choose which one to face as a priority. If your Attack Strength exceeds
that of the chosen adversary, you will inflict no injury on the other: you
will have dodged it. If, on the contrary, the second has superior Strength,
you will suffer an injury according to the usual rules.

   If you kill one of the two frogs, the combat continues against the other
according to normal rules.
