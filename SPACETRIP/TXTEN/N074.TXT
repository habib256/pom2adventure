T 074 The box of honour, and the rival

   The box smells of candied fruit. Below, the arena turns over: horned
beasts, nets, gongs, sand raked between each act.

   Two seats away, in full light, Bizz Lightspan is running his live show.
Square chin, moulded plastic suit, twelve camera operators in a half circle.
His helmet is so chrome he uses it as a mirror to check his parting.

   "And here it is, my forty million friends, the planet Mungo!"

   On your right, a neighbour in a vinyl suit and white boots has not taken
her eyes off the purple hanging for one second.
