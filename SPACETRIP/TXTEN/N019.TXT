T 019 They gave you everything already

   Hans Lonely pats the bottom of an empty sack and lifts one shoulder. "He
only had the one. Come back in six months, or never."

   Chewpaca raises his left paw, turns it round twice to show that it is
fine now, and growls three syllables. Hans Lonely translates without
looking up: "He says hello."

   The band has started again. It is still the same tune.
