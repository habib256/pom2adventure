T 047 The transporter and the boot

   The duty technician nudges three sliders, blows on a console and swears
it will come back. Something makes a tired percolator noise.

technician shrugs: "Colour settles by itself. Usually."

   You film the green boot. Four followers declare it the best thing your
channel has ever posted. They may well be right.
