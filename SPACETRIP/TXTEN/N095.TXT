T 095 The Sponsor Shows Himself

   The last shot: the adventuress in the vinyl suit, white boots, one hand on
her hip, an explosion behind her, and you in the middle, extremely pleased
with yourself.

   -- Five, says the Sponsor. Five of five. The contract is yours.

   Behind the Convertible, out in the vacuum, an impossible sound: a machine
grinding, wheezing, insisting. A blue cabin appears where there was nothing,
lands on nothing, and opens its door onto a light that is far too warm.

   A man steps out. Long scarf. Pleased with himself.

   -- I am the Vortex Sponsor. Hello. They call me Doctor Which. You have not
won a million followers. You have won a trip.
