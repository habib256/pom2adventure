   You unscrew the Holophone battery and slide it into his assembly: a
broken record player, an umbrella and a circular saw. Nobody knows why it
works. It works.

   The finger blazes. The storeroom turns red. A tiny voice comes out of the
umbrella, very far away, very clear, and says something in a language made
of flutes.

   The small grey fellow starts to cry, presses against you and holds his
lit finger above both your heads: the best lighting of your career.
