T 010 The Cantina of Mos Ugly

   The door breathes out warm air that smells of fuel, wet fur and regret.
It is dark in exactly the way that defeats every camera ever built.

   A three-horn band plays the only tune it knows. At the bar a four-armed
barman lines up glasses. At a low table a smuggler in a waistcoat counts
banknotes across from a hairy copilot who growls. At the back, two
ridge-browed Klingers are looking for a reason. Under the VIP canopy,
twelve camera operators surround a chrome chin.

   Three Martian tourists photograph the napkin dispenser.

   The ring light of the Holophone comes on. The whole room turns round.
The whole room holds it against you.
