T 005 The outer sector

   Out here the charts go vague, then poetic, then wrong. Two suns argue
over the horizon of a planet that has no horizon.

   Four beacons all the same. The starship Surprise, exploring things
nobody asked to have explored. Arrakoss, a planet made of sand and nothing
else. Station CAL 9000, whose computer always answers politely. And Mungo,
where a goateed emperor runs arena fights and expects presents.

   The Convertible is waiting for a heading.
