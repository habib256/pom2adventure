T 080 The Pixel Invaders
V 081

   The road to the Orbital Dump crosses a debris field where the Pixel
Invaders live: small square green things that fly in tight ranks and have
only ever learned one manoeuvre.

   They drop one notch, slide all together towards one edge, and when the
rank touches that edge they drop another notch and set off THE OTHER WAY.
Always. Forever. They have no second idea.

   Ahead of you the rank has just touched the LEFT edge of your
windscreen. It drops a notch. It is about to set off again.

   One second to pick your dodge. The Holophone is filming. Of course the
Holophone is filming.
