T 056 Pompatarte's House

   After a path through fields and winding streets, you arrive at the
threshold of Pompatarte's house. You knock; the Goblin opens the door. She
leads you to the library where Pompatarte is seated, in robes.

   "So, how are our affairs going?" he launches immediately. "Have you brought
me a map to reach Courbensaule?" The question is direct; he awaits a precise
answer.
