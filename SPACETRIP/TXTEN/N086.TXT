T 086 Trolley takes it all back

   Trolley looks at your bins. Trolley looks at its binoculars. Trolley
looks at the bins once more, in case the universe has quietly corrected
itself.

   It tips everything onto the ground with the gentleness of a librarian
reshelving a book, and starts again. It is not angry with you. It does not
know how to be angry. That is almost worse.

   It taps the three bins again and waits. It will wait for ever.
