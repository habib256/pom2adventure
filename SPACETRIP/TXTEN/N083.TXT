T 083 The wellness pod
V 089

   The pod must once have been a luxury orbital spa. It is now flat as a
pancake, with a diamond-shaped door and background music still playing for
nobody at all.

   Inside, everything is broken: the jets, the benches, the cucumber
fountain. Everything except one heated cupboard that held, nobody knows
how, and that contains a bath towel. Clean. Folded. Warm.

   You take it. At the time it seems ridiculous.

   You have not met Mopey yet.
