T 002 The cockpit of the Convertible
V 003

   The roof shuts with the clack of a biscuit tin. The star chart lights
up and paints your face violet. Two sectors are blinking: the inner one,
warm and crowded, and the outer one, where the charts say only HERE,
PROBABLY.

   You clip the Holophone onto its arm. The ring light comes on. You are
live. You were already live, in fact, and have been for twenty minutes:
your twelve followers watched you lose a fight with a seat belt.

   A message arrives. Bizz Lightspan, in close-up, a selfie behind him: a
black helmet breathing like a radiator. "ONE down, everybody! Four to go!"
Forty million hearts scroll past.

   You have a Convertible, a telephone and nerve. That is a start.
