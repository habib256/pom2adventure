T 064 One, four, nine

   "On a moon very far from here," the soft voice says, "they found a black
slab. It is perfect. Its three sides are in the ratio one, four, nine.
Exactly. Nobody has ever known why."

   "I know why. And if you know too, then we have something in common, and I
shall open this door."

   The red eye tilts by a hair.

   "One, four, nine. What is the next number?"
