T 011 The three glasses

   The four-armed barman sets three glasses in front of you. The first
smokes. The second freezes and cracks. The third sings, all on its own,
one held note, slightly sad.

   "I do not serve strangers," he says. "But I serve people who listen.
The smoking one is for whoever already has fire. The freezing one is for
whoever is already cold. The singing one is for whoever talks all day long
with nobody answering. Take yours. Take wrong, and I do not step in."

   At the back a Klinger belches a small blue flame. Beside you the
Martian tourists shiver inside their heated suits, saying "Ack ack" in a
voice that is chattering its teeth.

   You have been talking to a telephone for six years.
