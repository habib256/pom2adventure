T 056 The Worm already tagged you

   You stamp your heel. The dune heaves, the Worm rises, recognises you,
shuts its jaw and regards you with what, in a worm, passes for polite
weariness.

   It turns around very slowly, exactly like a celebrity who has already
signed one autograph, and dives back into the sand.

   Sand rains down for a good minute. You have it in your pockets, in your
collar, all over the Holophone.
