T 035 The Shuttle Passage

   Right door. At the end of a short passage, the escape shuttle, running
lights on. Ripleigh is already at the hatch, hand on the control, Joanesy
under one arm.

   The ceiling tubes flicker. A pause.

   Between you and the shuttle, something unfolds: two and a half metres of
smooth black, a long faceless head, and a jaw that produces a second jaw,
like a drawer.

   -- Do. Not. Film, says Ripleigh.

   You think about the ring light. You think about your twelve followers. You
think this would be the finest selfie in the universe.
