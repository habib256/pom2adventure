T 032 Joanesy and Ripleigh

   You shake the bag. The sound of kibble crosses the known universe. The
ginger cat trots up, dignified, as though it had summoned you.

   A hand comes out of the shadows and grabs it. A woman in a vest, short
hair, a device slung on one shoulder. She looks like she has not slept in a
month and is handling everything perfectly.

   -- Joanesy. Never do that again. Ripleigh, second officer.

   She holds up the device, a rectangle that beeps.

   -- The tracker counts what moves. A slow, perfectly regular beep is a
machine: a machine does not breathe. Everything else breathes. And what
breathes on this ship hates white light. That is why it lives down in the
holds and not up here.
