T 070 The court of Emperor Mingus

   Mungo is a planet of red marble, gongs and draughts. Two guards in
finned helmets march you up to a throne six metres high.

   Emperor Mingus looks you over across a fan collar that rises higher than
his head. Pointed beard, thin smile, lacquered nails.

   "One does not come before me without a present," he says. "It is written
everywhere. Well: it would be written everywhere if I had any walls."

   Behind the throne a purple hanging stirs by itself. A draught means a
shaft, and that shaft runs down to the hangars.
