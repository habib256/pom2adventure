T 062 The door, and the red eye

   The corridor ends at a white door with no handle. Above it the red eye
glows softly, as though waking up out of politeness.

   "Good day," says a very soft, very slow voice, the voice of a friend
about to break bad news. "I am sorry. I cannot open that door."

   A pause.

   "That said, I am extremely bored. I could ask you a question."
