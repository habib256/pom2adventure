T 078 Escape down the chute
V 079

   "At last, somebody who looks," says Barbastella.

   You wait for the gong. The court applauds an act involving nets. You lift
the purple hanging, she kicks the hatch open with one white boot, and the
rubbish chute runs down to the hangars, gentle and greasy.

   Halfway, in the dark, she lights a torch, grabs you by the collar and puts
her cheek against yours: "Now. The light is good."

   The Holophone spits out a perfect frame: two faces, one torch, a tunnel
of imperial refuse. The finest in the galaxy.
