T 044 The captain plays the scene

   The wrong door opens onto a rest room. A man in a yellow tunic stands
there, fists on hips, chin out. It is the captain.

   "You. Have. Failed." He takes three steps. "But. I will. Show you. How.
One. Takes it." He grips your shoulder and makes you play the scene again.
Three times. Every line in three pieces. The camera is running.

   After an hour the officer with the pointed ears has you beamed outside.
Out of mercy, he says. Out of logic too.
