T 004 The inner sector

   The inner sector: trade lanes, stations that smell of fried grease, and
enough witnesses to make any small mistake go galactic before you have
finished making it.

   Four beacons answer your ping. The Cantina of Mos Ugly, which serves a
drink the law calls fuel. The Gloom Star, an artificial moon that is not a
moon. The freighter Nostalgo, silent for eleven months. And the Orbital
Dump, where the universe throws away what it regrets.

   The Convertible is waiting for a heading.
