T 034 Stone Choice

   Which Magic Stone do you use against the POOL BEAST?

   WITHERING: slows and weakens the creature, useful for control. FIRE:
inflicts immediate damage but may reveal your position. TERROR: risks
panicking the beast; variable effect depending on target.

   Warning: using it consumes the chosen Stone; check your inventory.

CU FLETRISSURE 237 USE WITHERING
CU FEU 291 USE FIRE
CU TERREUR 356 USE TERROR
C 209 Use nothing and change approach
