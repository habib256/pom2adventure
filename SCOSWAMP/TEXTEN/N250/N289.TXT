T 289 Tancred's Flying Horse

   The Forest Guards told you about "Tancred's Flying Horse". You decide to
spend the night there. You pay 1 Gold Piece for the room, then go up to bed
and fall asleep as soon as your head touches the pillow.

   In the middle of the night, however, a burning sensation on your finger
wakes you abruptly. The Copper Ring is warning you that danger threatens. You
then jump from your bed and seize your sword; at that moment, a shadow flees
the room. You then double-lock the door and go back to bed.

   When you wake in the morning, you feel well rested and you recover 2
STAMINA points. Alas! your night thief took two possessions from your bag,
starting with any Magic Stones lying on top. The Copper Ring remained on your
finger. You now leave the inn to resume your path. Test your Luck.
