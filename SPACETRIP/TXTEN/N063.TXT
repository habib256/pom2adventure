T 063 Three hours with Mopey

   He starts with his manufacture. He moves on to his thirty-seven thousand
colleagues, the taste of metal, the fundamental pointlessness of corridors.

   You film. After forty minutes there is not one viewer left on the live
feed. After an hour you fall asleep standing up. After three hours he
concludes: "There. And that was the short version."

   Two small articulated arms carry you politely to the airlock and set you
outside, without resentment, tagged "listener, exhausted".
