T 040 In orbit of the starship Surprise

   It is huge and white, a saucer in front, two tubes behind. Next to it the
Convertible looks like a toaster with fins. You open the channel and wave
grandly, smile number three.

   A very calm voice answers without rising a semitone: "Surprise here.
Identify yourself. Unaccredited visits are, statistically, a waste of
everybody's time."

   Twelve followers hold their breath. Eleven, really: your mother is doing
the dishes.
