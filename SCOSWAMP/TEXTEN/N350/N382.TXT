T 382 Return to the Quicksand
VR 270 421

   You realize you've returned to the Quicksand clearing, but this time, you
won't let yourself be surprised. You have two solutions if you wish to resort
to magic.

   The first consists of throwing an Ice Stone. Thus, the Quicksand will
freeze and you can cross it, but the ice will melt quickly behind you.

   The second solution consists of throwing a Growth Stone to make nearby
plants grow. These will then cover the Sand's surface forming a sort of path
on which you can walk safely. This path will remain there permanently and you
can therefore use it again if you ever pass through this clearing again. If
you have one or the other of these Magic Stones, you can make use of it.

MU QUICKSAND.MB
CU GLACE 270 Freeze the Sand with an Ice Stone
CU CROISSANCE 421 Grow a path with a Growth Stone
C 190 Try to jump over the Sand
C 223 Turn back
