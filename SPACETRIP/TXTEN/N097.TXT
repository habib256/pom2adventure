   The feed scrolls. The Sponsor says nothing for a very long time. It is
worse than being laughed at.

   -- One is missing, he says at last. The Great Challenge asks for five. You
have fewer. I could look the other way, but a contract is a contract, and I
have quite enough trouble with rules as it is.

   -- Come back when the feed is complete. I will be here. I am always here:
it is my principal failing.

   The screen goes dark. The cockpit smells of toast.
