V 329 230

   Progressing south, the croaking of thousands of frogs accompanies you. The
path leads to a clearing dotted with ponds.

   Huge mushrooms stand in the middle; a small man is sitting on one of them.
He is small and corpulent, with black eyes and twitching eyelids. His mouth is
abnormally wide; two enormous frogs guard him. A silver amulet in the shape of
a frog hangs from his neck and glows faintly.

   What do you do?
