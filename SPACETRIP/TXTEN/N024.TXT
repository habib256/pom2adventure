T 024 Sorted

   The hatch opens onto a throat of hot metal and blades. You have just
enough time to say "smash that subscribe button" before going in feet first.

   The Gloom Star grinder is a conscientious machine. It does not kill: it
sorts. Three minutes later it spits you out of the ejection duct, flattened,
wrung, folded in four and perfectly clean, inside a bag labelled ORGANIC
WASTE -- NOT HAZARDOUS.

   The bag drifts off into space. The Holophone never stopped filming. The
episode is now called "Fifteen Minutes In A Bag".

C 007 Drift with as much dignity as possible
