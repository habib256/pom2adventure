T 253 Dwarves' Potion

   You drink the potion and immediately experience a strange sensation. The
mixture was intended to "make beautiful" whoever drinks it, but it was
prepared for dwarves.

   Its effect on you is unusual: for an hour you're smaller and stockier than
usual, and your nose becomes noticeably larger.

   After an hour you will regain your normal appearance. During your next
combat, your SKILL will count as 1 point lower in your Attack Rounds. This
temporary effect will cease when the combat ends.
