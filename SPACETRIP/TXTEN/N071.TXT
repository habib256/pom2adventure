T 071 Mingus recognises you

   "Ah!" cries the Emperor, waving two tired fingers. "The little one with
the vial!"

   He checks nothing. An emperor never checks: that is the whole principle
of empire. Your Holophone is returned to you, and so is your seat.

   Behind the throne the purple hanging still stirs by itself, and the
draught smells of hangar.
