T 028 He Recognises You And Sighs

   The door opens onto the black sphere. The chair turns slowly, with all the
weight of destiny behind it.

   -- You again. Kuh-hooo.

   Lord Vapour looks at your Holophone, then at you, then at the ceiling.

   -- You already have the shot. It is online. My mother has seen it. High
Command has seen it. People keep asking whether I am all right. Kuh-hooo.

   He sits back down. The cape settles. Somewhere inside the helmet you hear,
very distinctly, the sigh of a radiator giving up.
