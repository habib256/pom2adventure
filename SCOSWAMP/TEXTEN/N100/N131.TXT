T 131 The Bird Mistress

   The Parrot stares at you with a round eye. "Come closer," it says, "she
knows you're coming."

   You pass through a curtain of palm trees and discover a splendid clearing,
populated by thousands of birds with shimmering feathers. Eagles and Herons
walk freely.

   In the center, a magnificent woman sits on a pedestal. It's the Bird
Mistress. She greets you and calls you by your name.

   "Which sorcerer do you serve?" she asks.
