T 089 The bin is empty

   You go back over your own steps and there is nothing left to take.

   The heated cupboard in the pod stands open and empty. The white armour
is a stack of plates you piled up yourself. Trolley has already sorted
everything, twice, for the sake of its conscience.

   It waves its binoculars at you. In a dump you never find the same thing
twice: that is the one rule of the place, and it is also the definition of
a dump.
