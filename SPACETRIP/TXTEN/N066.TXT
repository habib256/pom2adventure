T 066 The storeroom, and what stirs under it

   Crates, air filters, a bin of old cable. And, under a silver tarpaulin, a
small thing that breathes.

   It puts its head out. A metre tall, grey, round-bellied, with enormous sad
eyes. It raises one very long finger whose tip lights up like a night lamp,
and points it at the ceiling.

   "Home," it says.

   Then it points at the ring light of your Holophone, then at its own
finger, and repeats the gesture. It wants to phone. It is short of power.
