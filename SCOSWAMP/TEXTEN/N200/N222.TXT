T 222 Stratagus's Demon

   An enormous horned demon surges between you and the sorcerer. Green fur,
repulsive warts, gnarled hands like roots. Stratagus points a dry finger:
"Kill this fool and bring me his ring." You've been reckless; you must pay the
price.

   Impossible to flee: it would catch you. It has no weapon, only its
monstrous fists: a chance remains to defeat it.
