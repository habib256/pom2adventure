   You push the ring light to maximum. The passage goes white.

   The creature freezes, then takes a step back, head turned away, one long
six-fingered hand raised in front of its face -- exactly like anybody leaving
a nightclub at seven in the morning.

   That is your second. You slip under its arm, you frame, you smile. It does
not. It has nothing to smile with.

   The shot is magnificent: the double jaw open, the drool catching the
light, and you in front with the face of someone having a very bad day.

   -- NOW! shouts Ripleigh.
