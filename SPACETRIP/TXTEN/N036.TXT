T 036 Selfie In The Dark

   You kill everything: no sound, no light, just the sensor. A blind creature
will never see it coming, you reason, with admirable confidence.

   It is not blind. It is simply badly lit, like everyone else here. In the
dark, it is at home.

   The photograph exists. It shows: black, black, a row of translucent teeth,
black, and one finger -- yours -- in perfect focus.

   It carries you by the belt to the outer airlock and puts you down outside
the way you put out the bins. Ripleigh scoops you up. She says nothing at
all. That is worse.

C 007 Float about until the autopilot arrives
