T 025 The Meditation Chamber

   The door opens onto a black empty sphere. At its centre, a chair. In the
chair, an enormous silhouette: cape, black metal helmet, a respirator wheezing
like an old radiator. Kuh-hooo. Kuh-hooo.

   -- I have been expecting you, says Lord Vapour. Kuh-hooo. Twelve followers
is twelve more than anyone gives me credit for.

   He agrees to the selfie. He stands, arranges the cape, finds his angle.

   -- One condition. Kuh-hooo. No. Flash. I have lived in the dark for forty
years. Flash reminds me of unpleasant things.

   The room is very dark. Your ring light hesitates.
