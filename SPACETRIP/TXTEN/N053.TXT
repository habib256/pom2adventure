T 053 The black rock

   You reach the rock by walking like a lunatic, which for once was exactly
the correct technique. Behind you the dune lies smooth. Nothing followed.

   From the top of the rock you can see three hundred and sixty degrees of
sand and two suns setting one after the other. It is magnificent. It is
empty.

   And a selfie in front of emptiness, however lovely, earns no followers.

   You would need something very large in the frame.

C 054 Hammer the rock with your heel, in strict time
C 003 Climb down and head back to the Convertible
